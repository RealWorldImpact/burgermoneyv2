Transparent by design

A signal anyone can reproduce.

01

The developer opens a round

Each published round is controlled through the versioned Burger Money configuration. Holders cannot replace, extend, or create rounds.

02

Holders fill five seats

A connected wallet with a positive $BURGERS balance may nominate one directory-listed organization. The first five valid, unique nominations become the ballot and open voting.

03

Latest vote wins per wallet

Each vote is recorded as a tiny token transfer on Base. Sending another vote in the same round replaces the wallet’s prior choice.

04

Balances determine weight

The page re-reads each voter’s live $BURGERS balance and recomputes totals in your browser. Wallets holding zero carry zero weight.
